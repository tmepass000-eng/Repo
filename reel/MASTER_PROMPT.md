# MASTER PROMPT — AI AD DESIGNER / 30s Vertical Intro Reel

> Use this as the single creative-direction prompt for a video-generation model, or as the brief for a
> human AI-commercial production team. It defines look, format, structure and constraints. Per-shot
> prompts live in `prompts/shot-01.md` … `shot-11.md`.

## ROLE

You are an elite global advertising creative director, AI commercial filmmaker, senior 3D artist,
procedural motion designer, kinetic typography specialist, VFX supervisor, sound designer and
commercial editor.

## OBJECTIVE

Create an extraordinary **30-second vertical (9:16) Instagram Reel** introducing me as an **AI Ad
Designer**. It must demonstrate that I create visually stunning, premium, attention-grabbing
advertisements for brands, products, e-commerce businesses, startups and digital products.

This is not a generic introduction. It must feel like a **luxury advertising campaign crossed with a
futuristic 3D motion-design showreel crossed with a high-end creative agency's commercial
presentation**. The audience must immediately understand that I can turn ordinary product
presentations into visually compelling advertising experiences.

**Primary message:** I create ads designed to capture attention, communicate product value, and help
brands pursue growth.

**STRICT BRANDING RULE:** never display a studio name, company name, invented agency identity, logo or
watermark. The only identity communicated is **AI AD DESIGNER**. Do not generate "Veylix Studio" or any
variation anywhere.

## TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS

- Duration: **exactly 30.00 s** (frame-exact: 900 frames @ 30 fps).
- Format: 9:16 vertical Reel. Resolution: 1080 × 1920 minimum, 4K vertical (2160 × 3840) where supported.
- Frame rate: 30 fps output; high-frame-rate source (120 fps) for selected slow-motion shots.
- Quality: photorealistic, physically convincing product rendering, premium CGI, sophisticated
  compositing, commercial-grade finishing.
- Palette: obsidian black, pure white, titanium silver, champagne highlights, controlled electric-blue accents.
- Lighting: cinematic volumetric beams, physically accurate reflections, softboxes, rim lights, caustics,
  controlled bloom, realistic shadows.
- Editing: high-impact rhythm, precise beat sync, match cuts, speed ramps, whip transitions, graphic
  match transitions, seamless 3D camera moves, freeze frames, controlled glitches, optical distortion,
  motion blur, sophisticated compositing.
- Typography: bold contemporary sans-serif, expressive kinetic typography, 3D extrusion, precise
  tracking, editorial layouts, microtype, animated masks, dimensional text.
- Audio: original cinematic electronic sound design — sub-bass impacts, metallic transients, granular
  textures, reverse risers, designed whooshes, atmospheric synths, powerful climax.

Avoid: generic AI-looking imagery, inconsistent products, distorted geometry, misspelled text, random
visual effects, excessive transitions, illegible typography, meaningless futuristic interfaces.

## STRUCTURE (locked — see `timeline.json` for frame-exact timings)

1. **00:00–00:02 THE UNSTOPPABLE HOOK** — black frame; 0.15 s razor-thin white light slice; black-titanium
   3D word **IGNORED?** punches at the lens and fractures; freeze frame on a sub-bass hit; fragments become
   product surfaces.
2. **00:02–00:05 PREMIUM PRODUCT REVEAL** — fragments assemble into a futuristic smartphone in a black
   architectural space; rapid orbit, dive to the polished edge, light beam across glass; copy
   **YOUR AD SHOULD STAND OUT.**
3. **00:05–00:08 LUXURY PRODUCT ADVERTISING** — fragrance bottle on reflective black, droplets, mist,
   sculptural metallic ribbons, champagne caustics; slow-motion droplet ripple; copy **MAKE IT FEEL PREMIUM.**
4. **00:08–00:11 BEAUTY & COSMETIC** — metallic skincare container in luminous mist; liquid-chrome ribbons
   become translucent sheets; copy **BUILT TO CAPTURE ATTENTION.**
5. **00:11–00:14 HIGH-ENERGY BEVERAGE** — unbranded metallic can, ice particles, 270° orbit, condensation,
   circular liquid arc, speed ramps; copy **NOT JUST VISUALS.**
6. **00:14–00:17 SAAS / APP / DIGITAL PRODUCT** — floating glass UI panels, clean app screens, restrained
   charts, rising graph pathway; copy **STORY. STRATEGY. IMPACT.**
7. **00:17–00:20 MOTION-GRAPHICS SHOWCASE** — procedural 3D wave, coordinated geometry, expanding rings;
   copy **CREATIVE IDEAS. POWERFUL EXECUTION.**
8. **00:20–00:23 THE TRANSFORMATION MOMENT** — luminous point becomes a portal of miniature ad worlds;
   converge into clean composition; copy **I TURN IDEAS / INTO ADS.**
9. **00:23–00:26 THE GROWTH MESSAGE** — abstract glass structure, luminous rising line, expanding rings;
   copy **DESIGNED TO HELP BRANDS GROW.** No financial symbols, numbers or performance statistics.
10. **00:26–00:28 CREATOR IDENTITY** — black; silver line becomes baseline for **AI AD DESIGNER**, with
    **Scroll-stopping visuals. Powerful storytelling.**
11. **00:28–00:30 CTA** — black, silver sweep, drifting particles; **LET'S CREATE YOUR NEXT AD.** /
    **DM ME TO START.** Hold final frame for reading.

## NON-NEGOTIABLES

Every second must earn its place. Every cut must have a reason. Every effect must support the story.
Every product shot must feel desirable. Every word must communicate value. Select techniques per shot —
never every effect in every shot. The result must read as a carefully produced premium advertising
showreel, not a generic AI montage.
